Screenshot Translation recognizes text in a selected screen area and translates it into your target language.

## How to use it

1. **Add a shortcut.** Open `Shortcut` from the sidebar and add a shortcut for Screenshot Translation.
2. **Download an OCR model.** Under `Setting > Screenshot > OCR models`, download a model for the language you need to recognize. EasyChat provides OCR models for more than 80 languages.
3. **Capture and translate.** Press the configured shortcut and drag over the area to translate. EasyChat recognizes the text and displays the translation on screen.

## Requirements and display modes

1. At least one working translation source, either an AI model or machine translation, must be configured first.
2. Choose a result layout under `Setting > Result Window > Result Window Mode`:
   - **Classic:** shows the translation at the top of the screen for quick reading.
   - **Dictionary:** opens a floating dictionary window near the pointer with details such as phonetics, parts of speech, definitions, examples, and pronunciation. Dictionary details require an AI model.
