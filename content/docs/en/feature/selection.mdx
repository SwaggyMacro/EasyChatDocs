## How to use it

1. Under `Setting > Selection`, enable `Enable Selection Toolbar` and choose the actions you need:
   - **Translate:** translate the selection into a chosen target language.
   - **Summary:** produce a short summary of the selection.
   - **Polish:** improve fluency and style.
   - **Correct:** correct grammar and wording.
   - **Explain:** explain the meaning or context.
2. Select text in another application. When the toolbar appears, choose an action.

Except for general translation, these actions require an AI model.

## Trigger and application scope

`Selection Method` provides three trigger modes:

- **Double-click:** show the toolbar when you double-click a word or phrase.
- **Press and hold:** show it after selecting text by holding the mouse button.
- **All:** enable both methods.

Selection actions can also be assigned on the `Shortcut` page. Shortcut actions remain available even when the automatic Selection Toolbar is disabled.

Use `Setting > Selection > Application List` to control where the automatic toolbar appears:

- In blacklist mode, the toolbar is disabled in listed applications and enabled elsewhere.
- In whitelist mode, the toolbar is enabled only in listed applications.

The application list does not restrict selection action shortcuts; global shortcuts continue to work in every application.
